# RecruitOps customer quick start

RecruitOps takes a candidate from sourcing through joining and invoicing. Sample records are labelled **Sample** and can be safely removed without affecting real data.

## Admin

1. Log in with the Admin account supplied by your organisation.
2. **Pipeline** shows every active candidate in the organisation. Use search, stage, and recruiter filters to focus the sheet.
3. Choose **Add candidate** for a blank record, or **Import CSV** for a reviewed bulk import.
4. Assign each candidate from the **Recruiter** column. Unassigned records remain visible only to Admins.
5. Open **Approvals** to approve or reject recruiter requests for protected-field corrections.
6. Open **Invoicing** to review Joined candidates by bank/NBFC, download the invoice CSV, and mark the set Invoiced.
7. Sample data can be removed from the top banner or **Settings → Sample data**. Type `REMOVE SAMPLE DATA` to confirm. Real records are untouched. Use **Restore sample data** in Settings to bring back the same batch without duplicates.

## Recruiter

1. Log in with your recruiter account. **My Pipeline** contains only candidates assigned to you.
2. Update operational fields directly: stage, next follow-up, last contact, notes, and document status.
3. For a protected detail such as phone, email, employer, role, fee, or owner, use the shield/correction action and submit a reason. Track it in **My Requests**.
4. Use the archive action when a record should leave the active pipeline; its history is retained.
5. Use the mail action to open a prepared email draft, then copy it into your email client.
6. Review **My Follow-ups** for assigned work that is due or overdue.

The **Coming Soon** panel previews planned capabilities only; those items are not active features.
